# Photo sources

The app loads illustrative home photos from Unsplash's image CDN at runtime. They are stock imagery only and do not depict the sample properties as actual HomeScout listings. Images have descriptive accessibility labels and fall back to a local color illustration when offline.

The backend's synthetic property examples use distinct images only as illustrative media, with an `is_illustrative` flag and explicit demo notice. They are never evidence of a property. Online images require a network connection; a failure falls back to a local color illustration.
